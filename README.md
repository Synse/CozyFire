CozyFire won't increase your Spirit, but it will improve your camping experience in WoW: Forever. It marks campfires on the world map and minimap and tooltips show the buffs each camp feature grants.

### Features

*   Automatically marks campfires when you place one, `/sit`, `/sleep`, `/lay`, or `/kneel` at a campfire
*   🆕**New in 0.8.0:** Campfire locations are shared with other CozyFire users in your guild/party
*   Tooltips for camping objects show their buff and profession information
*   🆕**New in 0.6.2:** Hold **Shift** to view level ranges for buffs that scale based on your level
*   Live countdown on each campfire pin showing the time left before it despawns
*   One-click minimap button to `/sit` at a nearby campfire
*   Pins persist between sessions and clear themselves once a fire is gone
